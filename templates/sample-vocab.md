## turtle | /ˈtɜː.təl/ | noun
- definition: loài bò sát có mai cứng bảo vệ thân, bơi lội hoặc bò chậm rãi
- example: The green sea turtle can migrate across entire oceans.
- forms: turtles
- tags: animals, nature

## tenacious | /təˈneɪ.ʃəs/ | adj
- definition: kiên trì, ngoan cường, bám sát mục tiêu đến cùng
- example: She was tenacious in defending her research results.
- forms: tenaciously, tenacity
- synonyms: persistent, determined
- tags: character, advanced

## compile | /kəmˈpaɪl/ | verb
- definition: dịch mã nguồn ngôn ngữ bậc cao thành mã máy hoặc mã trung gian
- example: You need to compile the source code before running the application.
- forms: compiles, compiled, compiling, compilation
- tags: dev, programming
